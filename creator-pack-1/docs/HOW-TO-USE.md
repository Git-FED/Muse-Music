# How to use

1. Choose a 60-second loop, 30-second cut, or ringtone from `tunes/`.
2. Drop the MP3 under your YouTube video or Short. You can also use the music in TikToks, Reels, podcasts, and streams under the license.
3. Mix the music below dialogue and trim or loop it as needed.
4. Keep `LICENSE.txt` and your receipt in your project folder.
