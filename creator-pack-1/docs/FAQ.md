# FAQ

**What am I buying?** Five original tunes with 60-second loops, 30-second cuts, and 20-second ringtones: 15 files total.

**Who is it for?** Creators making kids content who need friendly background music for videos.

**Can I monetize?** Yes, under the included license.

**Will you make a Content ID claim?** No claim from us for authorized uses. Keep the license and receipt.

**Can I resell or register the music?** No.

**Can I use it in an ad, TV show, or film?** Not under this standard creator license; request a separate license.
